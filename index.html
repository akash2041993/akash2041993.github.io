Questions

Good to know

Do you work with clients outside India?

Yes. We work online with brands and creators in the US, UK, Australia and India. Because of the time difference, we can often deliver overnight for clients in the US and UK.

Is the free sample really free?

Yes. We make 2 sample ads or 3 reels from your product or footage. If you like them, we talk about a monthly plan. If not, no problem.

What do you need from us?

Your product details or footage, your website or channel link, and any ads you have run before. For AI ads, product photos and key benefits are enough to start.

How fast do you deliver?

We agree on delivery dates before we start, based on how many videos you need, and we stick to them.

Do you only edit, or also shoot?

Both. We shoot in Indore with our own camera and drone, and we travel for shoots. We also edit footage you send us from anywhere.
